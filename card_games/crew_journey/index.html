    The Crew - Journey to the Ends of the Earth

    What is it

    Cooperative trick taking game where we're working through stages trying to accomplish goals!

    The stages are missions laid out in the log book. In each stage you play several rounds, and each round you must accomplish a goal. Goals are only completed once you've played all your cards

    Do we know what a trick taking game is?

    Talk about must follow, may trump

    Highest card of lead suit wins unless trump was played. Then highest trump

    Gameplay Overview

    This is a must follow game

    • Deck maekup
      • 4 suits 1-9
      • 1 trump suit 1-4

    The trump suit can be led whenever, and then you must follow with trump if able

    Communication tokens

    Normally, you can't talk about what is in your hand.

    We have a number of communication tokens equal to players that we're able to use every round to give information about what is in our hand.

    After set up, and before or after any trick (but never during!) any player may grab a communiction token from the center of the table

    • Put a color card from your hand on the table, and put the communication token in one of three positions to indicate information. Helicopters (trump) can't be used with communication tokens.
      • Top of the card: This signifies that the card on the table is your highest of that suit
      • Middle of the card: This signifies that the card on the table is your only card of that suit
      • Bottom of the card: This signifies that the card on the table is your lowest of that suit
    • This card still counts as being in your hand and must be played during the round, it is just public information now.
    • Put a reminder card in your hand so you don't forget about the card on the table
    • You can only communicate these 3 different pieces of information
    • This means there are cards in your hand that you may not be able to use a communication token for
      • Example, if you have a 3,4, and 5 of pink, you couldn't use the 4 to convey any info. Its not your highest, your lowest, or only card of that suit
    • You will never change the position of the token throughout the round.
    • Although we have one token per player, the same player can use multiple tokens during a round

    Stages and rounds

    (flip open to a map and put the person and timer on the board. Show a couple task card backs)

    Each stage consists of multiple rounds. We play rounds, or hands, until we've completed the stage. We're trying to get to the end of the trail before time runs out

    • The crew token represents our progress on the mission
    • The clock token represents time passing
    • We'll have a selection of 2 different tasks to complete
    • The number on the back will tell us how many spaces we'll move the crew token along the map if we successfully complete the task during the round
    • The higher the number on the card, the more difficult the task will be
    • The timer will always advance one space at the end of a round, regardless of us succeding or passing
    • Our goal is to get the crew marker to the end of the map before the timer token reaches it.

    Gameplay

    We'll decide which mission to do, then deal out all of the cards.

    We have the option of discarding ONE of the task cards and revealing a new one before we decide.

    • Person with the Helicopter 4 will take the first player token
    • They will lead the first trick
    • For all future tricks, whoever wins the trick will start the next trick.
    • After we play all the cards in our hand, we'll check to see if we completed the task or not.
    • We'll advance the clock one space
    • If we completed the task we'll move the crew marker the appropriate number of spaces
    • If we're not at the end of the map we'll pick a new task, shuffle and deal, refresh the communication tokens, play another round
      • If we reach the end before the clock does, we win and advance to the next stage
      • If the clock reaches the end before us (remember it moves before we do at the end of the round) then we fail the stage.

    There are different transportation symbols on the map spaces

    • A train space (what we see in the first mission) has no extra rules.
    • Later stages can have different spaces that have unique rules, we'll talk about them as they come up.
    • Some stages have different modifiers, but we start nice and simple.

    There is a scoring mechanic that we won't worry about, we're just vibing.
