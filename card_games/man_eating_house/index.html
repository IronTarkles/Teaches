    Man Eating House

    Overview

    We're all kids messing around in a haunted house. This is a must follow team trick taking game where your partner will be the person that sits across from you

    In this game special rules are introduced depending on what cards that are played into a trick. Don't worry, we've got a flow chart on a player aide card.

    Deck makeup

    Hold on to your butts because things are going to get weird

    • 3 suits 1-14
    • Green has a 0, that's the dog
    • Red has a 15, that's the old man
    • Each suit also has a number of children, a single litte girl, weapons, locations/danger zones, and ghosts

    Don't worry, the back of the rulebook will be in the middle of the table as a reminder!

    Gameplay overview

    We play over a single round, and the team that collects the most children and ghosts will win (those are the point cards)

    • The lead player will play whatever they want
    • Everyone else must follow suit if able
    • We'll check the flow chart to see if any special conditions have been triggered
    • If no special conditions have been triggered, highest card will win, regardless of suit
    • In the event of a tie, there is a suit heirarchy of Red > Yellow > Green
    • The winning player puts all the scoring cards face up in front of them separated into children and ghosts so that all players can see whats been played
    • All non scoring cards are discarded
    • The player who won starts the next trick

    There is a pile of cards in the middle of the table called the unopened room. The player who wins the final trick will win any point cards out of the unopened room.

    Events and special cards

    Here is where things get weird.

    Whenever we all play a card, we'll go down the flowchart for how the trick is actuall resolved.

    Look at your flow chart. Follow blue arrows for no, and red for yes

    • Was the first played card the little girl (a 4)? If so, do Kagome (don't worry about it for now)
    • If not, were the dog and old man borth played? If so, the dog wins
    • If not, was the old man played? If so, hte old man wins
    • If not, did a danger zone appear (a location with a red symbol on it)? If so, highest number wins
    • If not, was there an event (escape or struggle)? If so, cards involved in event are eliminated and the highest number wins
    • If not, the highest number wins

    So basically did something weird get triggered? If not, highest number wins, regardless of suit

    Events

    When certain cars are played into the trick, they can trigger an event.

    • Escape event
      • 2 or more children of the same suit were played
      • The children help each other escape! Each one goes into the scoring pile of the player who played it
      • The Dog (green 0) counts as a green child and will escape when another green child is played
      • Once the children have been removed, the highest remaining card wins the trick
    • Struggle event
      • When at least one ghost and one weapon of the same suit are played into the trick, there is a struggle!
      • If there are multiple ghosts and/or weapons of the same suit, all will particpate in the struggle
      • Highest numbered weapon scores all the ghosts in the trick, moving them in front of themselves
      • Weapons are then discarded and then trick winner is evaluated as usual

    Multiple events can happen in the same trick and are resolved separately.

    Special cards

    There are 4 special cards that can cancel events or change how a trick is won

    • Danger zone
      • Each suit has several location cards, of which specific ones with the red symbol on the are considered DANGER ZONES
      • Danger zones cancel any events that would occur and revert the trick to highest card wins
      • Danger zones will trigger regardless if they are played on suit or off suit
    • Old man
      • Highest single card (basically a trump card)
      • Will win the trick and capture all scoring cards
    • Dog
      • Counts as a green child
      • If played into the same suit as the old man, the dog will win
    • Little girl
      • If a little girl is played first into a trick, we pause the trick and perform a Kagome
      • The little girl card is set off to the side of the play area
      • That player then draws the top card of the unopened room deck
      • That player announces a suit and everyone must put a card face down in front of them of that suit if able
      • If not able, put any card face down
      • The little girl player then calls out how the cards will be passed
        • Left, right, or accross
      • The passed cards are added to players hands
      • The little girl player now starts a new trick
      • Little girl cards are always left to the side of the play area so everyone can see which have been played

    Game end

    We'll continue playing tricks until all cards have been played from our hands. Remember, the winner of the last trick gets all the unopened room cards.

    Teams combine their point cards. Most points win!
