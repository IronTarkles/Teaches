    North American Railways

    Overview

    19th century North America, we're investors trying to get rich off of early railroad companies expanding across the continent. At the end of the game the player with the most money wins!

    Gameplay overview

    Money is secret, keep it hidden at all times!

    We've got a block of share cards and a block of city cards that we'll be interacting with from the bottom up. So for each block, only the bottom card of each column is interacted with

    There is also a rail line for each company with a starting city. We'll be adding new cities to these over the course of the game

    Rounds are made up of a few phases

    1. Buying shares
    2. Adding cities to rail company routes
    3. Income based on shares that you own

    We'll play over a number of rounds until an end game trigger is met.

    We've all got handy player aides, they'll come in handy especially for the shares phase

    Shares phase

    Starting with the first player and continuing clockwise:

    • Choose a share at the bottom of a column
    • If the company has no director:
      • Collect the share card and pay any amount to found the company
      • Must be at least $1
      • Place this money next to the company's tokens
      • This money now belongs to the company! It will be how the company expands to new cities and gets more profitable!
      • Take one of the company's tokens to signal that you are the new director of the company
    • If you are the company's director
      • Pay a fixed price of $10
      • $5 goes to the company's capital, $5 goes to the bank
    • If another play is the director:
      • Propose a price. You must be able to pay this price.
      • The director chooses one of the following:
        • Allow you to purchase the share:
          • Collect the share, paying the proposed price
          • Half goes to company, half goes to bank
          • If this purchase results in you having equal or more shares than the Director, you become the new director by taking the company token from them
        • Director buys the share themselves
          • Director will collect the share and pay the proposed price
          • Half is payed to the company, half is paid to the player who initiated the offer
          • If this happens, you may chose a share card from a different company and try again, or choose to end your turn

    If you have no money on your turn, you can just get $2 and pass.

    The shares phase ends whenever each player has taken 1 action or when no share cards remain.

    Cities phase

    Starting with the first player and continuing clockwise:

    • Choose a company where you own at least 1 share
    • Select 1 city card at the bottom of any column
    • Pay the cost on the left side of the city form the chosen company's capital
      • You may not use your personal money
    • Add this city to the right of a company's rail line, extending it
    • Rotate one of your shares for this company 90 degrees to indicate that it has been used. You may not use it again this round
    • On your next turn in this phase you could use a different share to buy another city card, possibly even for the same company

    You can also choose to pass. It is a hard pass.

    Turns continue like this until everyone passes.

    Income

    There are no turns in this phase, everyone just earns income from companys where they hold shares

    • Calculate income for each company
      • Determine the value of the company's rail netowrk by totaling the vlaue on the right side of each card in it's rail line
      • Count all player held shares in the company and divide the rail network's value by this number. This gives the single share value
      • For each share you own in the company, get the single share value from the bank
      • If there is a remainder on the division, the director gets a bonus $1, then an additional $1 is added to the company's capital

    End of the game

    End of the game is triggered in the following ways:

    • At the start of the shares phase there are fewer shares remaining than the number of players
    • At the start of the city phase there are fewer shares remaining than the number of players
    • At the end of the city phase no cities were purchased

    When any of these conditions is met, there is a final income phase with one difference.

    Each player gains a route bonus from the bank. Total the number of shield symbols on all city cards in each company's network. Players gain $1 for every shield in a network for each share that they have

    So if a line had 7 shields and you had 3 shares in it, that is $21 dollars.
