## Overview

We're witches making medicine, that is what witches do. It's very important how much stuff you shove in the pot, too much and it becomes unstable.

This is a must follow trick taking game where we care about the total number cards in the trick add up to. We'll be collecting cards to score, but don't want to go over the target nubmer!

## Deck makeup

The deck is made up of 5 different suits, 0-7. Trump will rotate between rounds.

## Gameplay

With 2/3 players the target number is 12, with 4/5 players, the target number is 15

We'll shuffle the trump card indicators and flip the top card to reveal trump for the round

This is a must follow game. You can only play trump if you can't play the lead suit.

Whoever wins the trick will take a card to add to their score pile, but who wins is determined differently depending on how the trick plays out. The winner will take a single card and add it face up to their scoring pile and discard all other cards in the trick.

- If the total number value of all cards in the trick is under the target number:
    - Highest card of the lead suit (or highest trump if any were played) wins
    - Winner takes card of the lowest number value from the trick to their scoring pile
    - If there are multiple cards tied for lowest value, you choose which one
- If the total number value is higher than the target number:
    - The trick ends immediately (even if all players didn't get the chance to play)
    - The player who caused the bust wins the trick (even if not highest number or trumps were played)
    - That player takes the highest card from the trick to their socring pile
- If the total number is exactly the target number
    - The trick ends immediately (even if all players didn't get the chance to play)
    - The player who caused the trick to reach the taret number wins the trick (even if not highest number or trumps were played)
    - That player takes whatever card they want to their scoring pile.

No matter the result of the trick, the "winner" will lead the next trick

- We keep playing until someone has no cards in hand. All other players will discard all cards remaining in their hands.
- Cards will remain in players scoring pile between rounds
- We'll shuffle up all the discarded cards and deal out 5 to each player and start a new round.
- The revealed trump card will go to the bottom of the trump deck and we'll reveal the next one
- The player who took the last trick will start the next round

## Why do we care what cards we take?

At the end of 3 rounds, we'll examine our scoring piles. Whoever is the closest to the target number without going over wins

Now you may ask yourself, what happens when I suck at go over the target number halfway through the game?

Well at the end of the game, for every full set of cards in your scoring pile (1 of each suit), you can discard any one card from your scoring pile. This bonus can be triggered multiple times. So if you have 2 full sets, you can discard up to 2 cards. This is optional, you can discard a number of cards up to how many sets you have (including 0).
