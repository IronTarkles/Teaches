## Overview

Exploring dungeons is fun, but picking the right one can be tricky (get it, its a trick taking game)! Some have barely any monsters, and some are packed with adventurers eager to split the spoils! On top of that is the dungeon warden always lurking in the shadows. Makes sense why adventureres carry around torches!

This is a must follow trick taking game with a set trump where players will have to bid how many tricks they'll win. There is a bit of wiggle room on the bid, and ways to earn bonus points. We'll play over 3 rounds.

This is where we find out if anyone is unfamiliar with trick taking games.

## Gameplay

We'll all start aout at dungeon 0. Every trick you win will advance you one more dungeon (and yes they wrap around).

### Bidding

We'll deal out the cards and light a torch before we get started

- You will place a card from your hand face down in front of you as your torch.
- This is your bid, or how many tricks you think you will win this round. Think of it as predicting what dungeon you'll end up in.
- This card will not be part of your hand, but later on you will have the opportunity to replace it, so it could end up getting played

### Trick play

The lead player will play a card, and everyone must play the same suit card if they have one

- If a player leads with a dragon, other players can play whatever they want (even if they have dragons)
- Once everyone has played a card, we'll determine the winning number
    - Highest played dragon card will be the winning number (dragons are trump)
    - If no dragons were played, then the highest card of the lead suit is the winning number

You'll notice I said winning number a bunch

- All players who played a card with the winning number will simultaneously advance one dungeon forward.
- Winning number is regardless of suit. So if someone wins with a dragon 2, anyone else that played a 2 into that trick also moves
- If you are on dungeon 7, you'd move back to 1.

#### Spawn monsters

The player who played the lowest number in the trick becomes the dungeon warden for the trick and decides where monsters go.

- It is possible for the winning number to also be the lowest number
- In the event of a tie, later played card wins

On to monster placement

- The dungeon warden must select exactly one card of each color played into the trick
- Each of these cards will go under the matching number dungeon
- As cards stack up we'll make a row where all the values are still visible
- Cards will stay in dungeons for the whole round, and will increase how many points those dungeons are worth at the end of the round
- Discard any cards that weren't placed into the dungeon

When all cards played into a trick are different colors, the warden gets full control! They can place as many or as few cards as they like.

When we're done with monster placement, the dungeon warden will start the new trick.

## The final trick of the round

Before playing the last trick of the round, all players will draw their torch card back into hand

You will now have two cards in hand, but we will only play one more trick.

For this final trick, YOU DO NOT HAVE TO FOLLOW SUIT

## End of the round

PLayers simultaneously reveal the card left in their hand after the final trick. 

- If the number of the card matches the number dungeon that player ended up in, they place the card face down in front of them
    - It will be worth bonus points in scoring
- If the number of the card doesn't match where they ended up, they add the card face down to the dungeon of the matching number
    - This becomes a dropped torch that will increase the points that dungeon awards

So the way this works is that you have some wiggle room on the bid you made at the top of the round. If it is looking like you won't hit that bid, do what you can to keep a card with a value of where you may end up instead

### Scoring

- You score points for the dungeon where you ended the round
    - 2 points for each dropped torch there
    - 2 points for each dragon card
    - 1 point for each monster card of other colors
- If multiple players occupy the same dungeon, split this point total between them, rounding down
- Any players with their torch in front of them (correct bid) score a bonus 3 points

## New round

Move everyone back to 0, shuffle up and re-deal.

The player with the starting player card begins the new round

After 3 rounds, highest score wins!
