## Overview

We're members of a family trying who invested in an inn to get rich! We'll rob everyone and get ride of them after so the police don't catch on!

## Gameplay overview

We play over 2 seasons, each made up of a variable amount of rounds. Travelers who leave the hotel without being bribed or murdered in the first season will return in the second season

Each round is played over 3 phases

- Evening: Where travelers are welcomed into the hotel
- Night: Where players take their nefarious actions
- Morning: Clean up phase

The general flow of the game is bribing people to fill up your hand, killing people, and then burying the body to hide it and get their money. You'll have to build new buidlings when you run out of places to hide bodies, and you'll have to launder your money when your pockets start to get too full.

## Evening

The start player will send travelers to the different rooms of the inn.

- The entrance stack is face up. You'll draw the top card and decide where to place it (you can also see the top card of the deck)
- At the end of the round, players who still have a traveller in their room will get a bonus.
    - Good rule of thumb is if a card is likely to get taken, put it in an opponent's room
    - If you don't think anyone will take the card, put it in your room
- When we go through the entrance stack we'll shuffle the exit stack and make a new enterance stack. This begins the second season
- When the entrance stack runs out a second time, fill rooms as best you can and that will be the final round
- If there are fewer travellers than players left in the entrance stack in the second season, the game ends immediately

## Night

We'll each take 2 total actions in a round. Starting with the first player, we'll go clockwise peroforming a single action, then go around taking a second action.

Before we jump into the actions, lets cover a major mechanic of the game. 

- Each card has a color, and the main actions are all a different color
- Most of the time you will need to pay cards to take actions. The number on a card is the cost to interact with it.
- Any card can be used for any action, but will be discarded if their color doesn't match the action
- You will discard any cards that you use for an action that don't match the color. 
    - So if you use blue cards to perform the blue action, they'll be returned to your hand
    - If you use other color cards to take the blue action, they'd get discarded and now you need to acquire new cards to fuel further actions

ON TO THE ACTIONS

### Bribe

The blue action (cash). You'll bribe to take guests from the inn and add them to your hand

- Select a traveller staying in the inn
    - Note the rank of the card you have selected
    - Play a number of cards from  your hand equal to the rank of the card you selected
    - Blue cards you played go back to hand, with the rest getting discarded (peasants go back to the board)
    - Add the newly bribed card to your hand
- You may instead choose to bribe a peasant from the stack on the board. You can take 1 or 2 of them for your bribe action.

### Build an annex

The red action (trowel). This is how you'll build buildings for special effects and have more places to hide your bodies

- Annexes are the house shape on the bottom left of a card, and may have an ability that will be active once the annex is built
- To perform this action, select a card from hand with an annex on it that you want to build
    - Note the rank of the card you have selected
    - Play as many OTHER traveller cards from your hand equal to the rank of the card you selected
    - Red cards you played go back to hand, with the rest getting discarded (peasants go back to the board)
    - Put the newly built annex into your row of annexes in front of you (your player aide is your first one!)
    - From now on you have the passive ability detailed on the annex
    - No limit to the number you can build, and you can have duplicates

### Kill a guest

The Gray action (gun). This is how you'll kill guests, which you'll have to do before you can get their money

- Select a guest in the inn (not your hand) to kill. Any room or a peasant. Note the rank of the card selected
    - Play as many traveller cards from your hand equal to the rank of the card you selected
    - Gray cards you played go back to hand, with the rest getting discarded (pesants going back to the board)
    - Take the killed traveller from the inn and flip it over to its corpse side in front of you.
    - There is no limit to the number of corpses that you can have in front of you, but watch out for the police

### Bury a corpse

The purple action (coffin). This is how you'll bury guests to get their money and escape from suspicion

- Select one of the corpse cards in front of you
    - Play a number of cards from your hand equal to the rank of the card that you want to bury
    - Purple cards you played go back to hand, with the rest getting discarded (peasants back to board)
    - Take the corpse and put it under an annex that has room for it. The number on an annex indicates how many corpses can be hidden here
        - If you use your own annex, tuck the card under it, you gain all the money, moving your token on the money track
        - If you use an opponents annex, tuck the card under it, you both split the money, moving both your tokens on the track

### Money laundering

The money track caps at at 40! If you would go over, you just lose that money. That is an awfully suspicious amount of money to have.

- Instead of taking an action on your turn, you can pass and launder money
- Exchange 10 money from the track for a $10 check. You can do this as many times as you want
- You can instead exchange a check for $10 on the track. You can do this as many times as you want
- Passing doesn't mean you're out of the round, if you passed and laundered on your first action, you would still get a second action that round

## Morning

Once we've all taken 2 actions, the night is over and we move to the monring for clean up

- If there are any police (gray cards) remaining in the inn, there is an investigation
    - If you still have a corpse in front of you, you are forced to panic and pay the villiage gravedigger to take care of the bodies at $10 each! The gravedigger also accepts checks!
    - If you can't afford that, you pay what you can.
    - Either way all the bodies are removed from the game, not discarded
    - Either way, you DO NOT get the money on the corpse. Cost of doing business.
- Remaining travellers leave
    - For each room that you own that is still occupied come morning, you'll get $1
- Pay wages
    - For each card you have in hand, you must pay $1 to keep them loyal
    - Travellers and peasants don't accept checks, you need to have cash on hand to keep them
    - If you don't have enough to pay, they'll get discarded (to the exit stack or board for peasants)
- First player card moves clockwise
    - Pass the card then start a new round with the evening phase

## End of game

After the enterance stack runs out a second time, the game is over!

There is one final police investigation, whether there were police in the inn or not!

Scoring is pretty simple

- There are some level 3 annexes that can score you cash, you mark this on the money track (losing anything over $40 as usual)
- Add the value of all of your checks with the money track, and that is your final score!
