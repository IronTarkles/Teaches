## Overview

Don't get too greedy or you'll get screwed over!

Deck makeup is 1-13 in 4 suits at 4, 1-10 in 4 suits at 3

## Gameplay

- Must follow
- Lowest number wins
- Trump is any color other than the lead suit
- Ties are won by later played

So if only one color is played, the lowest card wins

If two or more colors are played, then the lowest card not of the lead suit wins. Ties are broken by who played later.

The winner of the trick takes all the cards from the trick to place in front of them.

### Placing cards

Cards placed in front of you are how you score points. You stack cards of the same color in columns of ascending numbers

When you win a trick resolve each card one by one starting with the smallest number. When resolving, choose one:

- Place face up as a score card
    - Place the card in front of you to build your score
    - Cards must be placed ascending (smallest to largest)
- Place face down as a penalty card
    - Place the card face down in front of you
    - The card will be -1 point

If you ever aquire a card smaller than your top face up card of that color's column, you can't pick either of those options.

You instead incur the "Oshaka Penalty". Place this card face up in front of you but turn it sideways. It will be worth negative points equal to its value.

## End of the round

After we've played all the tricks we'll add up our scores

- Highest value card in each column (the one on top)
- -1 for each face down penalty card
- minus the number value for each face up sideways card in front of you

## Second half of the game

We will shuffle up and deal again for a new round. There is one new rule in the 2nd round.

You can only score 3 colors positively. Once you have 3 colors face up in front of you, EVERY card of the 4th color will be put face up sideways (meaning you'll get minus points equal to the number value)
