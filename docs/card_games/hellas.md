## Overview

We're acient greeks rebuilding greece after returning home from war

The goal of the game is to build up villages and turn them into cities to score the most prestige.

Over 3 rounds we'll be taking turns performing different actions.

## Gameplay

In each round, players use their action markers to select an action on the action board.

- A round starts with the first player and proceeds clockwise
- The active player chooses one of their action markers (black cubes) and places it onto an empty space on the action board
- An action space with a marker already on it can't be chosen again during the current round
- There's 2 different types of actions
    - General action (the arrow)
        - An action that will affect all players
    - Selective action (Trianle)
        - An action performed only by the lead player
- Selected action are performed immediately
- The next player then takes their turn
- The round is over when we're all out of action cubes

### General actions 

These are the actions marked with an arrow. When someone picks on of these actions, everyone will get to do it.

- The lead player performs the action first
- In turn order (clockwise) the other players will take turns resolving the action as well.
- If you are unable to pay for the action, or just don't want to take it, you can just pass instead of doing anything.
