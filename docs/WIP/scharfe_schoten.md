## Overview

A trick taking game with suit heirarchy where we'll be predicting what suits we'll capture the most and least cards of.

We can see the back of opponents cards, so you'll have an idea of what you're up against.

## Setup

Everyone is going to get a token with a plus and minus on it for each suit, these are what we'll be using to show our predictions.

After we deal out cards, the leftover cards will form a pile of cards that we'll be able to take from over the course of the hand

We'll also be setting the suit ranking and the super trump numbers
