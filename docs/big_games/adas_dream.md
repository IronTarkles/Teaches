## Overview

In an alternate history where Charles Babbage accepts the offer of Ada Lovelace to assist with turning his analytical engine into the first ever computing machine; we play as her assistants tasked with building different prototype parts of this machine.

We've been given blueprints (our playerboards), contacts (our cards), and guidelines (objective tiles) to help turn Ada's Dream into a reality.

The game will be played over an undetermined number of rounds until someone has completed their machine by filling up all 9 dice placement spots on their playerboard.

## Round overview

On your turn, you'll decide which type of turn you'll take. A workshop turn, or an action turn.

### Workshop turn

In the middle of the main board is the workshop area. 

On a workshop turn you'll get new dice, trigger bonuses, and play cards from your hand.

- Grabbing a die
    - Select a workshop die and move it clockwise into the next space
    - The die will "push out" a die of equal or lower value, this is the die that you'll be taking for yourself
    - The pushed out die will move onto the bonus space connected to that dice group (with the die icon)
    - You'll gain that bonus, then move the die into your store on your board (three slots on the left)
    - If you don't have free space in the store on your board, you can't take a workshop turn
    - Center of the workshop shows how to modify dice
        - One coal to move a die an extra space clockwise
        - One coal to adjust any die in the workshop plus or minus one
        - You can do both of these once each on a workshop turn

If there is now any empty space in the workshop with no dice, draw and roll 3 dice from the bag to refill it.

Refilling a workshop space in this way also gains you the bonus of that workshop space. 

#### Workshop bonuses

- There are spaces to gain resources: Brass, coal and mmoney. 
- There's a space to draw cards from your deck.
- The magnifying glass is research and lets you go up one of the 4 research tracks along the outside of the board
- Lightbulb moves your innovation track up

#### Playing a card

After gaining bonuses and getting a die, you can play up to one card from your hand

- Some cards require you to have taken a die of a certain value to be able to play (show pips in top left)
- Some will require you to pay money equal to the value of the die that you grabbed (question mark with coins)
- If you meet the condition to play the card, resolve its effect then put it in your discard pile
- Whenever you go to draw a card and can't, you'll shuffle your discard to make a new deck
- You will only have 8 cards in your deck throughout the whole game. Getting new cards means you remove a card from your hand or discard pile (but never deck)

#### Check for bonus research

At the bottom of your board are slots where we'll have assignments tucked in later.

- Check the matching color slot of the die you grabbed
- If you have cards in that slot, go up on that color research track
- Each action area of the board has its own research track that all work similarly
    - When your marker reaches a breakthrough space (with the ! triangle), you gain the reward shown in the breakthrough space at the top of that track
    - In addition, some breakthrough spaces grant an advanced version of the corresponding action you'll get as a bonus after taking the normal action here on an action turn
    - Reaching the top of the track gets you an assignment scoring token, with the first one giving a player 2 points per assignment of that color and the others granting 1 point (all end game)

So in summary a workshop turn goes like this:

- Move a die on the board to push out one of equal or lower value
- Get the bonus of that space (and any now empty space)
- Put the die on your board (remember you can't take this type of turn if you have no room for the die)
- Play a card if you want, checking that you met the condition or payed for it
- Gain research track bumps if you have tucked cards matching your die color

### Action turn

The other type of turn you can take, where you'll take a die from the store on your playerboard and take the action in the matching color section on the main board. Gray dice can be used to select an action of your choice.

- Use a die from the store
    - Grab a die and put it in the mill area of your board (one of the empty spaces where a die can go)
- Complete the matching colored main action
- Compelte the advanced action (if you've unlocked with research in that color)
- Many actions will allow you to place disks from  your playerboard onto the main board
- Every removed disk unlocks a slot where you can later build a gear
- If an action lets you place a disk, but you can't, you can move a disk already on the board
    - The exception is if you're moving a disk to an objective, you can't move a disk that is qaulifying you for that objective
- At the end of the game, completed rows and columns will be evaluted by the equations that you've made and award points (dice are numbers, gears are operations so like 5 x 2 - 1)
