## Overview

We're running our own moon colonies by deckbuidling with a twist. We're building a central deck affects all of use. Things WILL go bad, and everything will start falling apart.

Once one player is wiped out, whoever has the most survivors is the winner.

## Gameplay overview

Someone draws the top card of the progress deck, and we all resolve the effect on the card.

We'll start adding cards to the deck, which will be added to the top of the deck.

Once the whole deck is flipped over and resovled, we'll shuffle up the discard pile and do it all over again.

## Cards in the basic deck

### Work

4 of the cards in the starting deck are "Work". These cards allow you to perform any one of the main actions:

When one of these cards is drawn, we'll put a reminder token over the lowest visible number on our board. This just helps you remember how many work cards are left in the deck.

How the actions work:

- Mine: Gains you 4 money
- Farm: Gains you 4 food
- Research: Draw 2 bulding cards
- Build: Play a building card from hand
    - Pay the money cost in the top left
    - Put it below your board
    - If it has a "play" effect, resolve it
    - While buidlings are in play, they may modify the matching colored action or give you a permanent passive ability
    - The people icon on the top right counts as people in your colony, but you don't grab a token to add to your people pool
- Restock: Gain 2 crates
    - These tokens have to be placed on buildings
    - They don't do anything by themselves, but other cards may have different effects based on the number of crates stored on the card

#### Effects

There's a wide range of effects on cards

- Some effects may tell you to add a perk
    - Choose one of your personal perk cards and add it to the top of the deck
    - When perk cards are drawn from the central deck, only the indicated player does a thing
- Some effects may tell you to add a specific card
    - These refer to development cards. Search the development deck for the indicated card and add it to the top of the deck

### Trouble

A card in the starting deck named trouble will add the top card of the event deck to the top of the central deck.

- Events will negatively effect everyone
- Eventually there will be events that start to add robots into the deck
    - We'll add the top card from the robot deck to the top of the central deck
    - Robots are even worse than events.
- Once robots start getting added, the events just continue to get worse and worse
- Basically we'll be trying to build our engines up as much as we can before things start crashing down around us

## Losing People

- Any time you lose people, you must lose them from your playerboard if you can
- If you don't have enough to lose from your board, you must lose a building instead.
    - If the building has a "lost" effect, you would then resolve it.
    - Add the number of people on the card to your board, then remove the indicated number of people you needed to lose for whatever effect started this
    - If you still couldn't pay the necesary number of people, continue losing buildings this way until you can pay

## Game end

The end of the game is triggered is after fully resolving a card any number of players are completely out of people and buildings.

The person will the most settlers (playerboard + cards) is the winner!
