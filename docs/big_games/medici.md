## Overview

We're buyers in a market competing for the goods coming in. We'll be auctioning off lots of goods over the course of 3 rounds, but our ships can only hold so much stuff!

## Boards overview

The main board is split up into five sections to track our different totals of goods obtained. In the center are tokens that show what round it is. The track around the outside tracks how much money we have, which is also victory points

Each player has a ship board in front of them with their boat on it. This is where your goods you win will go. If you ever have over 100 dollars, put your ship by the 0 space of the score track to indicate this.

## Gameplay

- We've got a set of cards for each good type 0-5, with 2 copies of 5 but one copy of everything else. 
- There is also a single value 10 gold card that doesn't correspond to any type of good.
- We'll shuffle these up and remove some cards based on playercount. The deck will be used for the round
- At the start of the game, a random player will get the start player marker

On your turn:

- Reveal a card from the top of the deck for the table to see
- You can reveal 1, 2, or 3 cards. Once you are done revealing as many as you want, these cards will be auctioned off as a lot
- Starting with the player to the left of the lead player, each person will get one opportunity to bid
- Minimum bid is one, subsequent bids must be higher
- You may also pass
- The person who initiated the auction will always act last

The player with the highest bid wins the lot of cards and loads them onto their ship after paying the amount of their bid, moving their score marker down by that amount

The first player card will now pass clockwise and we'll do it again

### Auction rules

- A won lot of goods is loaded onto a player's ship in its entirety
- Each card takes up a space on the ship
- You can never replace cards already on your ship
- So you can never bid on a lot that is more cards than you have empty space in your ship
- On your turn to start an auction, you can prepare a lot with more cards than you have space. You just wouldn't be able to bid on it.
- When your ship is full, you are out of the round, and play continues without you.
    - You will no longer bid or select lots
- If all players pass on a lot, the cards are discarded and the first player marker passes to the next player

## End of round

- Once all players but one have their ships loaded, the round ends
    - The last player then fills up their ship by taking cards directly from the top of the deck
    - These cards are free
- The round can also end if the deck runs out

### Scoring

- Players will earn money according to the total value of the goods that they've purchased
    - Add up the total numbers on all the goods in your ship
    - Players are paid out according to the grid on the payment card
    - Highest total gets the most money, lowest gests nothing
    - Ties see the tied places add up the amounts, divide evenly rounded down
- Count the number of purchases each player has made for each of the 5 goods
    - Number value on card doesn't matter, just the number of cards of a single type
    - So two green cards would move you up two on the green track
    - The gold ten card doesn't move you on any track
- After moving up on tracks, check who is highest
    - Highest player on each track gets 10 dollars
    - 2nd highest gets 5 dollars
    - Ties are resolved the same way
- Award track bonuses
    - A player that has moved into one of the spaces showing bonus points (5, 10, 20) will get this much money
    - These are resolved independantly, there are no ties. If a player is there, they get the amount no matter what (even if they weren't highest or 2nd highest on the track)

We'll resolve one good track at a time, flipping over the good token after scoring one to keep track of things.

After paying out everything, we'll set up for a new round.

- Flip goods tiles back over
- Remove a round counter
- Player pieces stay at their position on all the tracks
- All cards come back and get shuffled with those removed
- A new deck of the same amount of cards is created
- First player marker goes to the player with the lowest money/score

After 3 rounds, the highest score is the winner!
