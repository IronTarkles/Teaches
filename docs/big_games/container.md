## Overview

An economic game where we're producing, selling, buying, and auctioning off containers. Everyone has a secret end game card that tells them which containers are the money makers for them. The person with the most money at the end of the game is the winner!

## Game Flow

The general flow of the game is this:

Everyone is going to start with a colored factory that will produce a single container of that color

- You produce containers with factories, and price them in your factory district (lower priced side of player board)
- Other players buy containers from your factory disctrict and price them for sale in their harbor districts (higher priced side of player board)
- Players will move their boats to other player's harbor districts to buy the containers for sale there
- Loaded up boats are delivered to container island, where an auction will occur for the cargo on that ship.
- The winner of the auction gets those containers into their personal scoring area.
- At the end of the game you'll get points for different colored containers at different rates. Everyone has a different highest payout container color!

## Turn overview

On your turn, there are a few steps. we'll talk about actions first. On your turn, you'll take two actions. Some are limited to once per turn.

All of these are lisetd on your player aide

### Build

Building factories lets you produce more goods, building warehouses lets you hold more goods in the harbor

Grab the desired piece from the supply, and pay the cost of the next lowest price spot shown on your board. Money is paid to the supply.

You can never build more than one factory of the same color

### Produce

This is how you get more containers to sell. You can only do this action once per turn.

- First pay $1 to the player to your right
- You must produce 1 container of the matching color for each factory you have
- Set the price by putting the containers in a lot of the factory district. 
    - They don't have to go to the same lot 
    - You may also re-price containers already there.
- The limit of containers you may have in your factory district is determined by your number of built factories
    - 1 factory is a limit of 2 containers, 2 factories is a limit of 4, so on
- If you would produce containers above your limit, select which containers you want to produce and leave the rest in the supply
- You may not discard containers already on your board to make room for new ones

## Factory purchase

This is how you buy containers to put in your harbor district for players ships to come buy

- Chose a single opponent
- Select any number of containers from their factory disctrict to purchase. Pay them the total sum of containers you grab
- Move those containers from their factory district to your harbor district, setting the prices however you want
- You may also re-preice the containers already there
- The limit on the number of containers in your harbor disctrict is determined by how many warehouses you have built
- You can't discard containers already in your harbor to make room for newly purchased ones. If you're at capacity, you can't buy more.

## Harbor purchase

This is how you buy containers to load up on your ship. Your ship must be docked at an opponents harbor to take this action

- Buy any number of containers from the harbor district where your ship is docked, paying the indicated price for each container
- Place the containers on your ship in any order. 
    - You can hold a max of 5 containers
    - You can't discard containers already on the ship to make room for more
    - There is no way to increase you ship's capacity

## Reprice

This is how you can re-price things without producing or buying

- Chose one district on your board
- Freely move around any containers you want

Sure it's inefficient, but if you are at capacity in both districts you can take this action if you need to price your goods more competetively

## Sail

How you move boats around the board

Boats can only be in a few places:

- Docked at one of the two islands
- Docked at an opponents harbor (you can never dock at your own)
- In the open ocean

When you take this action you either

- Move your ship from one of the docking spots that we mentioned to the open ocean
- or from the open ocean to one of the docking spots
- You can't move directly from one docking spot to another with a single action

When your sail action is moving into the ocean, the action is done. It takes a total of 2 actions to move from one docking spot to another.

When your sail action takes you into a dock, you get to perform a docking action for free. So when you get to 

- Opponen'ts harbor: You get to take a harbor purchase action there as a free action
- Container island: You immediately hold a delivery auction
    - After this auction your turn ends, even if you hadn't taken a 2nd action yet
- Off shore bank: You can load any number of containers from your holding area onto your ship

## Delivery Auctions

This is your main method of acquiring containers to score at the end of the game

When you sail into container island, you immediately trigger an auction for all containers on that ship.

- All players besides the auctioning player will place money cards face down to form a secret bid
- Once everyone is ready, all players simultaneously reveal their cards
- Highest bid wins, losing bids get their money back
- There are $0 cards you can bluff with, and these are always returned to your hand (even if you win the bid)
- If there is a tie for the winning bid, a runoff auction is held
    - The tied players leave their revealed money on the table, retreive any $0 cards, and then make a second bid of facedown money that will be added to the first. If the runoff is also a tie, the owner of the ship decides the winner.


The player running the auction now has 2 options:

- Accept the bid, giving all containers on their ship to the winning player
    - The person that wins the bid puts all containers in their area on container island
    - The player running the auction gets the money from the winner, plus that same amount from the bank. This means you will always get double the price of the highest bid!
- Instead of accepting the bid, the player running the auction can instead buy the containers themselves
    - They pay money equal to the highest bid to the offshore bank
    - They take all the containers and put them in their own area on container island
    - The bidder that got cucked gets their money back to their hand

## Offshore bank (call bank action)

This board provides additional ways to exchange containers for cash or cash for containers

When you take this auction , if there is a bank auction token (yellow cylinder ) in the supply, you can start a new auction.

- Place the auction token on one of the six bid sites on the bank island. Ther are 3 for money and 3 for containers
- Grab the appropriate bid tile, put it in front of you with your opening bid on it.

If there is an auction token currently on a bid lot, you may outbid the person who currently has the highest bid there.

- The opponent gets their bid back and you take the tile in front of you, putting your bid on it

Bidding cash for containers:

- Use the cash bid tile (the one with boats on it)
- Use cash from your hand, putting it face up on the bid tile.
- If an opponent has the tile, you must bid at least $1 more than they did
- When you have the cash bid tile, you can't spend any of the cash you placed on the tile for any reason. 
- If an opponent outbids you, return the cash to your hand

Bidding containers for cash:

- Use the container bid tile (the street)
- Remove any number of containers from your districts to put on the bid tile
- Separate these containers on either side of the tile to indicate where they came from
- Put reserve tokens (little circles with containers) on your board in the factory and harbor lots where the containers came from 
- Only the number of containers you bid matters, not the color. If the opponent has the bid tile, you must bid one more container than they did
- If an opponent outbids you, return the containers to the disctricts they came from, but you can take the opportunity to reprice them. Return reserve tokens to the supply
- When bidding containers, the containers still count towards your district capacity. When checking capacity, count the reminder tokens as if they were containers.

In a 3/4 player game there can only be one active auction at a time. In a 5 player game, there can be 1 active cash auction and 1 active container auction at a time.

You can only use the call bank auction once per turn, and not on a turn in which you won an auction.

If you have the highest bid on an active bank auction at the start of your turn you win the auction.

- Pay the bank: distribute your bid across the cash or container lots as evenly as possible
- Collect won cash to your hand or won containers into your holding aread on the bank board
- Return the auction token and any reserve tokens on your board to the supply

Paying the bank

- You distribute stuff into the bank $1 or 1 container at a time
- Starting at 1, then 2, then 3. THen back to 1.
- Skip over any lots with a bid token on them.

## Loans

If you're short on cash, you can always get a loan as a free action at any time. Even on other players turns! Even during delivery auctions!

- When you request a loan, take $10 from the supply
- Take a bank loan card from the supply and put in near you
- You can have a max of 2 outstanding loans at any time.
- At the start of your turn, you must pay $1 interest to the offshore bank for each loan that you have
- You must pay interest before doing anything else on your turn (including winning an auction)
- If you can't pay interest for a loan, you must take out another loan to pay interest on it
     - If you already have 2 loans, you default
- You can pay back a loan at any time on your turn as a free action (after paying interest)
    - Pay $10 from your hand to the supply, and return the loan card to the supply
    - You must pay the full $10, regardless on how much interest you have paid
    - Outstanding loans will also be paid at the end of the game out of your victory money, plus $1 interest
- Defaulting
    - When you default, the offshore bank seizes 1 container from you for each loan you can't pay interest on
    - First taken from your scoring area on container island, then your ship, then hlding area on bank board, and so on. 

## Game end

When the supply runs out of any 2 colors of containers, the game ends at the end of the active player's turn.

If there are any active off shore bank auctions when the game ends, the player with the highest bid automatically wins them.

### Scoring

Everyone reveals their final scoring card and does the following

- Discards all of the most common color container in their scoring area
    - If there is a tie for the most, you decide which to discard (unless your two value container is one of the tied colors)
- Use your scoring card to determine the value of all your containers
    - The second price shows your two value container color
    - Each of your containers of this color are worth $10 if you have at least 1 container of each color in your scoring area (including the color discarded at the top of the scoring phase)
    - If you did not, these containers are worth their lower value.
    - If the two-value container is tied for your most common it must be discarded
- Score leftover containers
    - For each container of yours not on container island, get this much money from the supply
        - $3 per container on your ship and in your holding area in the offshore bank
        - $2 for each container in your harbor
        - $0 for each container in your factory
- Repay any loans at $11 each
