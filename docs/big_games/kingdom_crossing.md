## Overview

We're beaver formen trying to help gather resources to build a new bridge in the kingdom. The queen hates crossing the same bridge more than once in the same day, as it makes the other bridge owners jealous. Due to this, we don't have the authority to cross the same bridge more than once in a given day BECAUSE ITS THE LAW


We'll work tegether with the animal guilds of the forest and try to prove ourselves worthy of securing the queen's trust to build the new bridge

## Board overview

- The board where we'll be moving our player pieces around is split up into 4 islands, diveded by 7 bridges.
- Each of these islands specializes in producing a particular resource
    - There are resource tracks on your playerboard
- Each pile of cards specializes in doing a different thing

## Round overview

We play over 4 rounds split into 3 phases, morning (where we get income), noon, (where we take our actions), and night, which is a cleanup step

### Morning phase

We'll start of by getting income from 2 different places on your playerboard

- Top of your board
    - Where you'll be putting the majority of cards that you purchase
    - Cards you buy will have a guild they're associated with, that is the column that they'll get tucked under
    - You'll get different income based on the guild, but you'll only start getting it once you have 2 cards tucked in a slot
        - Fox guild gives you victory points
        - Owl guild gives you an invest action. There is an investment track in the bottom right of the board. An invest action lets you move your marker up OR get the money where your marker is and move it back to the start
        - Bear guild lets you trigger structures below your playerboard
        - Boar guild lets you upgrade your action tiles
        - Deer guild lets you gain a resource of your choice
- Bottom of your board
    - Where you'll be putting structures that you obtain
    - We all start with one that gives us an income of an invest action

Next we'll flip over bird tokens onto each card on their individual islands. These will be additional bonuses obtained when buying cards

Then we'll flip over the card in the queen deck on the top of the tree side board.

## Noon phase

This is the main part of the round where we will take turns performing a single action until we'll all done 4 actions for the round. Most actions will involve us taking bridges to different islands to purchase cards.

On your turn:

- Take one of your action tiles and decide to either
    - Rest: Take the amount of money shown at the top of the tile
    - Work: Move your beaver and resovle the action on the card
- Keep your action tiles on one side of your playerboard. 
    - No matter what you do with a tile on your turn, move it to the other side of your playerboard when you're done. 
    - It won't be able to be used again this round.
- Even though we've got 6 action tiles, you'll only be taking 4 actions in a round

When you choose to work,most of your action tiles show a little paw print with a number in it. This is how many bridges you must cross when taking your action.

- As a reminder, you are never allowed to cross over a bridge more than once in a round
- For every bridge you cross, you'll put one of your player color paw print tokens on it to show that you've used it this round
- The number on your action tile is the required amount of bridges you must cross
- You are then required to purchase a card in the location where you end your turn
    - This means that if you can't take an action that would end your turn somewhere that you can't afford a card
    - So you'd either have to take a different action or use that tile to rest
- Some cards have a red coin cost in the middle, and some cards are free
- When obtaining a card with a bird tile, gain the bonus on it before resolving the card
- Gain any bonuses shown on the card, then tuck it under the appropriate guild at the top of your board
    - Structures have a building tag instead of a guild tag in the top right and are tucked below your board
        - Structures will also always only come from 2 specific stacks of cards, they are not mixed in all the stacks
        - Asides from gaining you an immediate bonus, you'll also gain their bonus as income every round
    - Some cards bonus will show something with a times marker and then the pawprint icon
        - This means gain X for how many bridges you've crossed so far this round (can tell by how many tokens you have out)
        - For example a card that showed coins x 2 pawprint would mean gain 2 coins for every 2 bridges that you've crossed
